About BloodLink

What is BloodLink?

BloodLink is a mobile platform designed to simplify blood discovery and connect people with available blood donors. Instead of depending only on traditional methods of searching for blood, BloodLink provides a convenient digital platform where users can discover blood listings, find donors and connect with people nearby.

  • Easy donor discovery
  • Location-based search
  • Blood group filtering
  • Availability information
  • Simple communication
  • Community-focused platform
