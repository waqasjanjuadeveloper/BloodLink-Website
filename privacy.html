VeinX Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

VeinX is designed to help people find blood donors and blood listings more easily. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our mobile application and website, including account information, location data, app usage and user support.

2. Account information

To create an account or profile, we may collect basic information such as your name, email address, phone number, blood group and location details. This information helps connect users with relevant donors or requests in a useful and secure way.

3. Location information

VeinX may use location information to help users find nearby donors or relevant blood listings. This may include approximate location data when the app is in use or when the user chooses to share location-related information. Users can control or limit this sharing based on their device settings and app permissions.

4. App usage

We may collect information about how the app is used, such as app interactions, feature usage, crash reports and other diagnostic information. This helps improve the experience, performance, reliability and safety of the service.

5. Firebase services

VeinX may use Firebase or similar backend services to support app functionality such as authentication, real-time data, notifications, analytics and error logging. These services process data in accordance with their own platform terms and security practices. Please review Firebase documentation for additional details where relevant.

6. Data security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse or loss. However, no system can guarantee complete security, and users should exercise caution when sharing sensitive health-related information.

7. User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have the right to access, correct, delete or request information about their personal data. If you would like to update your data or ask a question related to privacy, you can contact us using the details below.

8. Contact information

If you have questions about this policy or would like to exercise your rights, please contact us at waqasjanjua.developer@gmail.com. Legal requirements may vary by jurisdiction, and we recommend confirming local obligations before making any formal legal claim.
